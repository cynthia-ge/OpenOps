# Repository instructions

- Describe OpenOps as a standalone product in code, comments, documentation, prompts and metadata. Do not name, link to, compare with, or record checkout paths or revisions of other codebases used during development.
- Keep product capabilities, limitations and validation results accurate. Dataset and saved-result provenance remain part of the application contract.
- Never add `Co-Authored-By` trailers. Check full commit messages before committing and verify task-created or amended commits before pushing.
